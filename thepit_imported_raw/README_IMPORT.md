## The Aether type
Types are matched by NAME, so the hack's new types are carried through and
given fresh constants after the vanilla range:
- `TYPE_AETHER = 21`  (ROM name "Aether")

To finish wiring a new type: add the constant, bump `NUMBER_OF_MON_TYPES`,
add its `gTypesInfo` entry (scaffold in types_info_imported.h), and use the
full `gTypeEffectivenessTableImported` (already includes its matchups).

## What is DATA vs ENGINE CODE
- Base stats, typing, dex #, moves' battle stats, descriptions, learnsets,
  type chart, sprites, palettes: pure data -> emitted ready to paste.
- Move ANIMATIONS + their SOUND EFFECTS: bytecode (`ase`). Serialized to
  text/bin under scripts/move_animations/. The sound commands (`playse`,
  `panse_*`) are also collected into scripts/move_sounds.csv.
- Move 'code': each move's gen-3 .effect selects a battle script (`bse`),
  serialized under scripts/battle_effects/. The `.effect` field in
  moves_info_imported.h is a best-effort mapping; anything emitted as
  `EFFECT_PLACEHOLDER` needs you to port the corresponding battle script.
- ABILITY 'code': in gen 3 (and expansion) ability behavior is C in the
  battle engine, NOT data. Only id/name/description are extractable. Use the
  preserved FireRed ability indices (manifest.json) to find and port the
  logic into src/battle_util.c.

## Caveats
- Generated SPECIES_/MOVE_/ABILITY_ constants may collide with constants
  already in your project; reconcile names/indices before building.
- Sprite PNGs are indexed to the extracted 16-colour palette; the build's
  gbagfx step converts them to .4bpp. Adjust .smol/.gbapal extensions in
  graphics_registration.h to match your project's graphics rules.
- gen-3 secondaryEffectChance and move targets/effects sometimes have no
  1:1 expansion equivalent; these are flagged with comments.
